# Sound assets

The sound effects in this directory are adapted from Kenney's **Sci-fi
Sounds**, **Digital Audio**, and **RPG Audio** packs:

- https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
- https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio
- https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio

The source packs were created by Kenney (https://kenney.nl) and released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The selected source files were converted from Ogg Vorbis to MP3 and normalized
for in-game playback. Attribution is not required, but is included here with
thanks to Kenney.
